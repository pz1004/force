# FORCE follow up revision plan

**Implemented on 6 November 2026.** All four stages below are complete. The
findings and reproduction descriptions retain the pre-revision evidence.
The final suite passes **416 tests in 13.10 seconds** with one numerical thread.
Implementation details and smoke results are recorded in
[VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md#quantile-arithmetic-validity-and-online-target-follow-up).

Revise the current working tree to repair quantile arithmetic and prevent invalid or selectively successful estimates from being presented as unconditional accuracy results. Preserve the formal default estimator in Equations 6–13, the declared exact-prefix readout, and the existing delayed-window schedule. Matching historical timing or RMSE values is not an acceptance criterion.

This follow-up is based on the working tree reviewed on 6 November 2026. Its existing suite passes **322 tests in 12.96 seconds** with one numerical thread. The reproductions below expose cases that suite does not cover. The reference is the local [FORCE paper](mathematics-14-00191.pdf), SHA-256 `a44f2a3a7e085b40f979e1260c6a977f1c71e64e499295bd5b3c9908cc17afac`.

This plan addresses the remaining review findings. The earlier [algorithm revision plan](ALGORITHM_REVISION_PLAN.md) remains the historical implementation record; its completed numerical, replay and streaming work should not be repeated.

## Review findings

| Finding | Current evidence | Required change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 Quantile arithmetic loses representable results | `_quantiles.py` still overflows in the P² parabolic candidate on `linspace(-1,1,1000)*1e306`. A scalar median tracker fed `[-maxfloat, maxfloat]` returns `-inf` instead of zero. An exact-prefix 25th percentile can also overflow on `[-M,-M,M,M,M]`, although the answer is `-M`. | Repair P² and exact linear interpolation, with finite-result checks and independent arithmetic references. |
| R2 Undefined correlations receive accuracy scores | Batch and online diagnostics on `ones((60,3))` report completed execution and approximation RMSE zero, although every pair has zero variance. Benchmark RMSE likewise ignores pair-status metadata. | Separate execution success, estimability and scoring coverage. Never score a zero-filled undefined coefficient as an estimated zero. |
| R3 A failed warm-up suppresses unattempted order replicates | `run_benchmark.py` caches a failure on original-order `X` and labels every randomized replicate failed without fitting it. Real FORCE inputs can fail on that order and succeed on requested permutations. | Separate warm-up diagnostics from measured-replicate outcomes and preserve meaningful timing initialization. |
| R4 Order summaries omit selection by success | `_summary_records` drops failed orders, then labels the remaining mean/interval as error over random orders. Requested and failed counts are absent from the per-method summary. | Report the attempted population and explicit success conditioning; do not infer all-order accuracy from successful orders alone. |

The revised `exact_prefix` support closes the earlier optional-mode gap. Exact readouts through the prefix, background ordinary P², buffer release after the transition, and batch/replay parity are a defensible interpretation of the paper's unspecified handoff. Preserve the disclosed possible discontinuity and absence of improved later P² markers.

The new order-replicate identities, conditional-order interpretation, paired timing joins, genomics reference provenance, and archival exporter label are appropriate changes. The remaining reporting issues concern failures and undefined results, not the basic replicate design.

## Governing statistical decisions

| Topic | Decision for this revision |
| --- | --- |
| Online coverage | Retain delayed previous-window thresholds as the supported Remark 9 alternative. Exponential weighting and mini-batch merging remain separate future methods. |
| Public undefined-pair representation | Preserve the documented zero-filled matrix for compatibility. Require validity-aware scientific readouts and reporting. An optional NaN view may be added, but an API-breaking default change is not necessary to repair R2. |
| Negative estimated IQR | Keep its current warned collapse-to-zero behavior explicitly identified as a compatibility policy. Do not describe this as a genuine zero IQR. Scientific diagnostics must flag affected bounds and disclose or exclude affected pairs under the declared comparison policy. |
| Other quantile crossings | Diagnose all adjacent crossings among the five quantiles, allowing ties. Do not silently sort or project the estimates. Broader rejection is an explicit policy change. |
| Genuine zero IQR | Retain Equation 7 and its point acceptance interval. Do not introduce scale floors, widen bounds, or substitute MAD. |
| Correlation validity | Require at least two accepted observations and positive accepted variances. Two nonconstant observations give an algebraically defined coefficient even though evidence is weak. A unit diagonal remains Algorithm 1's output convention, with marginal validity reported separately. |
| Online target | Retain cumulative historical acceptance. Strengthen its positive definition and validation; do not claim it estimates current-regime correlation or automatically inherits Proposition 1. |
| Paper inconsistencies | Preserve standard P² ranks and pair-specific means. Do not add a 25% breakdown guarantee, clean-correlation identity or root-N claim that the paper's argument does not establish. |

Changing the default bound policy to strict rejection or online quarantine is not required to fix the confirmed findings. If pursued later, batch/replay can reject invalid bounds without publishing a new fit. Online quarantine must specify which next-window pairs receive no updates while quantile learning continues, record excluded exposure, and include that validity-dependent selection in its target. Reusing older bounds is a different schedule. These changes must not enter indirectly through a reporting repair.

## Stage 1 Repair quantile arithmetic

Files: `src/force/_quantiles.py`, exact-readout call sites in `core.py` and `trimmed_pearson.py`, and numerical/hybrid/state tests.

1. Evaluate the P² parabolic candidate using scaled or otherwise overflow-safe differences and ratios. Preserve Equation 4, the direction and spacing conditions, and the ordinary marker-order test that selects Equation 5.
2. Make the linear fallback safe too. An overflowing implementation intermediate is not a reason to choose a different mathematical update. Truly unsupported results must still fail explicitly and invalidate partially updated state.
3. Provide safe linear-quantile interpolation for provisional scalar readouts, exact-prefix readouts and the exact batch paths. Preserve endpoint order statistics when the interpolation weight is zero or one. Handle opposite-sign finite endpoints without first forming an overflowing difference.
4. Validate final readouts so finite input cannot silently produce a successful `NaN` or infinity. Keep empty-tracker readiness semantics distinct from an arithmetic failure.
5. Retain the same prefix boundary, background marker history, chunk invariance, buffer release and default pure-P² behavior.

Acceptance criteria:

- The default two-column fit at scale `1e306` succeeds for the reviewed fixture; its intended bounds near plus/minus `2.23e306` remain finite.
- The failing P² update is checked independently with Decimal arithmetic. Ordinary, reversed, tied and rescaled streams cover all five target probabilities.
- The median of `[-M,M]` is zero for provisional readouts, and the exact 25th percentile of `[-M,-M,M,M,M]` is `-M`; neither silently returns infinity nor raises because of avoidable interpolation overflow.
- Scalar, batch and replay agree for matching modes and input order. Existing ordinary-range tests remain within justified floating-point tolerances.
- Tests do not impose whole-estimator scale invariance across the specified absolute-epsilon branch or require permutation invariance from P².

## Stage 2 Make scientific results aware of validity

Files: `_moments.py`, `core.py`, `streaming.py`, `diagnostics.py`, `estimators.py` where baseline validity must be exposed, benchmark/convergence runners, reporting and figure consumers.

1. Define a shared result contract distinguishing numerical execution status, pair estimability and bound-policy diagnostics. Use existing compact pair codes rather than allocating string matrices during fitting.
2. Have independent reference calculations return their own counts and variance-validity masks. Do not copy the production mask into an oracle. Exact trimmed comparators and baseline outputs also need explicit validity information; a finite matrix alone does not establish estimability.
3. Define the eligible pair universe from the named reference before scoring. Record total eligible pairs, each method's defined pairs, bound-policy problems, compared pairs and coverage. Record the comparison mask or a reproducible identity for it.
4. For the main benchmark, require complete validity over the declared pair universe for the primary full-matrix RMSE. If coverage is incomplete, return an unavailable primary metric with a reason. A separately labeled partial metric may use a common comparison mask across methods, with its mask and coverage reported.
5. For pairwise algorithm diagnostics, calculate approximation error only on pairs defined by both the production estimate and its reference, and label it as conditional on that comparison set. An empty set produces a null metric and an explicit no-comparable-pairs status, never zero error.
6. Surface all-five quantile crossings. Track crossed-IQR policy events separately from true zero IQR and from accepted-pair zero variance. Preserve API compatibility while preventing these cases from being presented as clean successes.
7. Keep execution completion separate from a statistically complete estimate. Update CSV/JSON and figure validation together. Strict JSON uses null for unavailable metrics. Eigenvalues of a compatibility zero-filled matrix must not be presented as evidence that all correlations were estimable.

Acceptance criteria:

- Constant-input batch and online reproductions have no valid off-diagonal accuracy metric, despite successfully executing the declared output convention.
- Tests cover zero, one and two accepted observations; constant accepted samples; mixed valid/invalid features; genuine zero IQR; and crossed quartiles.
- The real crossing fixture `default_rng(0).standard_cauchy((50,2000))[:,437]`, paired with `linspace(-1,1,50)`, reports the bound-policy issue and no ordinary accuracy score for its undefined pair.
- A method cannot improve its primary benchmark score by returning more undefined pairs. Partial comparisons use an explicit common set, with coverage visible.
- Fully defined existing results retain their coefficients and scores. Compatibility zero filling remains distinguishable from estimated zero correlation throughout exported reports.

## Stage 3 Correct order replicate failures and summaries

Files: `scripts/run_benchmark.py`, `tests/test_benchmark_alignment.py`, reporting and benchmark figure consumers.

1. Record warm-up outcomes separately. Failure on an original-order throwaway fit must not fabricate failures for requested permutations that were never attempted.
2. Ensure timed runs have an explicit warm-up state. Compile or warm compatible numerical paths outside timing; if this cannot be established, mark timing unavailable or not warmed rather than interpreting compilation time as ordinary fit time. Estimation success and timing eligibility are distinct.
3. Attempt every requested replicate whose input/protocol is valid, preserving its original identity. Record actual fit, validity and timing outcomes without retrying failures until success.
4. Summarize requested, attempted, successful, failed, incomplete and timing-eligible counts, with clear denominators. Include methods with zero successful fits in the summary.
5. If any requested order lacks a valid score, do not report the successful subset as an unconditional mean over orders. Report the unconditional metric unavailable and, if useful, a separately named success-conditioned mean and interval with counts. These remain conditional on the fixed dataset and reference.
6. Preserve identity-matched paired timing tests and their excluded-pair counts. State success/timing conditioning explicitly. Do not impute zero errors or timings for failures, and retain the existing distinction between fixed-order repetitions and order replicates.

Acceptance criteria:

- A fixture whose original-order warm-up fails but some requested permutations succeed produces actual per-replicate results. Test this control flow with a deterministic order-dependent estimator so it remains meaningful after Stage 1 repairs the current numeric trigger.
- A ten-order fixture with five usable and five failed results reports all ten attempts, five failures, and an explicitly conditioned five-result summary. It cannot claim an unconditional zero mean/interval merely because successful errors are zero.
- All-failed methods remain visible. Missing results, duplicate identities and mismatched data/reference/order identities are rejected or counted according to the documented contract.
- Existing shared permutations, seed reproducibility, unchanged reference, method interleaving and paired joins remain intact.

Current real-data reproduction for the control-flow defect: set `x=linspace(-1,1,50)`, replace its last ten entries with `1e306`, duplicate it into two columns, and permute rows using `default_rng(5)`. Original-order FORCE warm-up fails, while requested order replicates 1 and 4 at seed 7 succeed when fitted directly. The runner currently labels all five requested fits failed. Without the initial seed-5 permutation, ten requested orders at seed 7 contain five successes and five failures, but the current summary describes only the five successes as the order mean.

## Stage 4 Specify and validate the online target

Files: `README.md`, `VERIFICATION.md`, the streaming class documentation, `diagnostics.py` and focused online diagnostic tests. Keep the implemented schedule unchanged.

The finite-stream target is Pearson correlation over post-calibration observations with historical weights `w[t,i,jk] = 1{X[t,i] lies in the bounds learned from window t-1}`. Counts, means and co-moments are pair-specific and cumulative. Preserve the documented calibration exclusion and the same historical decisions across arbitrary input chunks.

For an iid stream and fixed logical window size B, let Z be an independent calibration block, and let A_B,jk(Z) be the pair's region under the actual declared bound policy. Define:

```text
g_B,jk(x) = E_Z[1{x is in A_B,jk(Z)}]
dF_B,jk(x) = g_B,jk(x) dF_jk(x) / E_F[g_B,jk(X)]
```

Assume the calibration rule supplies finite bounds almost surely, acceptance probability is positive, selected moments are finite, and selected variances are positive. Then the pooled accepted moments target correlation under F_B,jk. This is a derivation from the schedule, not an extra theorem supplied by the paper. Adjacent block contributions share one calibration/evaluation block, so they are dependent. Serial dependence requires assumptions about the joint process; drift need not admit a single stationary target. A terminating arithmetic failure with positive per-window probability precludes an unconditional indefinitely running estimator; finite-sample successes alone do not establish this limit.

The current warned collapse-to-zero policy is part of A_B,jk. If a future mode skips invalid windows, include its validity indicator inside g_B,jk. If it reuses older bounds, revise the state/target derivation instead of retaining the one-window expression unchanged.

Validation should use three distinct references:

1. An independent finite-stream ledger of thresholds, acceptance, counts and moments for the exact implemented schedule.
2. Exact quantiles within the same windows, to assess the effect of the approximate quantile backend without changing calibration or accumulation.
3. Independent calibration blocks and evaluation observations, pooling accepted moments to approximate the stationary target. Report Monte Carlo uncertainty from independent complete replicates; do not average window correlations or apply naive iid rowwise uncertainty formulas. Report calibration failures explicitly rather than silently discarding them and claiming an unconditional target.

These references must distinguish random fixed-window bounds from deterministic population bounds. Increasing the total number of windows can stabilize the historical-selection estimate without making each window's quantile error vanish. Preserve the existing qualifications on Proposition 1, clean correlation, root-N rates, drift adaptation and positive semidefiniteness.

## Execution and completion

Implement Stage 1 and Stage 2 as separate reviewable changes. Stage 3 depends on Stage 2's validity/status contract; Stage 4 documents and validates the final declared policy. Preserve the exact-prefix tests and the revised order-replicate/provenance tests throughout.

Run the relevant regressions after each change, then the full suite and small batch/replay/online/benchmark report checks after integration. Exercise reports with complete, partially defined, all-undefined and failed-order cases. Check JSON/CSV round trips and that figures cannot hide missing coverage or failure conditioning. Recheck bounded replay/online memory if new masks or diagnostics alter allocations.

Completion requires all four reproduced defects to be covered and repaired, explicit metric eligibility and failure denominators, a documented online target under stated assumptions, and preservation of the existing default formulas and successful prefix/replay behavior. It does not require MAD, drift handling, every Remark 9 alternative, quantile rearrangement, PSD projection, new marker reseeding, or reproduction of historical table values.
